Pre-production

Development, planning and paperwork, handled before the first day of shooting.

  • Development and script
  • Casting from our own database
  • Location scouting
  • Storyboarding and previz
  • Budgeting and scheduling
  • Line production and permits

Also: talent management liaison, self-tape direction, background and extras.

Production

Cameras, crews and support for anything from a doc unit to a full TVC shoot.

  • Latest Sony cinema and field cameras
  • Full prime and zoom optics inventory
  • Gimbals for run-and-gun work
  • Steadicam, dolly and crane support
  • Cinema lighting and grip packages
  • Wireless audio and transmission

Also: reportage and doc units, multi-cam social shoots, broadcast and TVC crews.

Post-production

Editorial, grading and mastering in our own suites, on reference monitors.

  • Three editing rooms
  • Offline and online editorial
  • Color grading on reference monitors
  • Theatrical projector finishing
  • Mastering for DCP, broadcast and web
  • Remote-working servers

Also: secure cloud review, versioned dailies, cross-site project sync.

VFX and animation

Compositing, CG and motion design, supervised from set through to delivery.

  • Compositing and clean-up
  • CG integration and matte painting
  • On-set VFX supervision
  • Title sequences and broadcast packages
  • 2D, 3D, cut-out and mixed-media animation
  • Social-native motion design

Sound and stop-motion

Score, mix and handmade animation, from puppet builds to Dolby Atmos.

  • Original score and music supervision
  • Foley, ADR and voiceover
  • 5.1, 7.1 and Dolby Atmos mixing
  • Puppet and armature builds
  • Miniature sets and frame-accurate rigs
  • Hybrid live-action and stop-motion
